# JaxStats Codebase Overview (Nontechnical)

## What is JaxStats?
JaxStats is a web application that helps League of Legends players analyze and compare their in-game performance. It provides easy-to-understand statistics, insights, and comparisons based on recent match history. The goal is to help players improve by giving them clear feedback and visual summaries of their gameplay.

## What Can You Do With JaxStats?
- **View Your Stats:** Enter your League of Legends summoner name and region to see your recent match statistics, including win rate, KDA (kills/deaths/assists), vision score, and more.
- **See Champion Performance:** Get a breakdown of how you perform with different champions, including your win rate and average stats for each one.
- **Match History:** Browse through your recent matches, with details on each game's outcome, your performance, and key stats.
- **Compare With Others:** Use the comparison feature to see how your stats stack up against another player. This side-by-side view makes it easy to spot strengths and areas for improvement.

## How Does It Work For the User?
1. **Home Page:** You start on a simple, clean web page. There is a form where you can enter your summoner name and select your region.
2. **Get Your Stats:** After submitting the form, the app quickly shows your stats using previously saved data (if available), so you don't have to wait.
3. **Live Updates:** While you view your stats, the app works in the background to fetch the latest data. If new matches are found, your stats update automatically.
4. **Compare Tab:** You can switch to the "Compare" tab to enter two summoner names and regions. The app will show a side-by-side comparison of both players' stats.
5. **Visual Feedback:** The app uses clear cards, tables, and color coding to make stats easy to read and understand, even if you aren't familiar with all the game terms.

## What Makes JaxStats User-Friendly?
- **Fast Feedback:** You see your stats right away, even if the app is still updating in the background.
- **Simple Interface:** The design is clean and focused on what matters—your performance and how to improve.
- **No Technical Knowledge Needed:** You don't need to know anything about programming or data analysis. Just enter your name and region.
- **Helpful Comparisons:** The compare feature is great for friends, rivals, or anyone who wants to see how they measure up.

## Who Is This For?
- League of Legends players who want to track and improve their performance.
- Friends who want to compare their stats in a fun, visual way.
- Anyone curious about their gameplay trends and strengths.
